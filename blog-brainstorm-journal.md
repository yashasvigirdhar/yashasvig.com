## Post 1: Why I Left Google (June 2025)

### Raw Ideas & Thoughts:

**Facts/Context:**
- Left Google in June 2025
- Worked at Google for 5+ years
- Total ~10 years of software engineering experience
- Role at Google: Tech Lead/Senior Software Engineer on WearOS team

**Reasons for leaving:**
- Combination of different things led to the decision
- REASON 1: Freedom of time
  - Took unpaid leave for a month in 2023 (TODO: confirm exact year)
  - Realized how beautiful it is to have freedom of time in your control

- REASON 2: Fitness transformation journey
  - Went through my fitness transformation journey (as we call it) in 2024
  - Started January 2024
  - Starting point: Generally fit, never been obese, but been a bit skinny all my life - didn't have all these muscles and strength
  - Initial motivation: No health issues or wake-up calls - it started with the thought that I always wanted visible abs at least once in my life. Also wanted to progress in calisthenics goals (number of pull ups etc.) which had been stagnant for long at that time around 6-7
  - Went until April 2025
  - Had a sub-10% body fat fitness photoshoot of my own
  - The fitness photoshoot in April 2025 was one of the most difficult and rewarding things I've ever done in my life
  - Being almost perpetually a little hungry for 4 months, leading to this event
  - Balancing fitness prep with full-time Google job was quite hard:
    - For a few months, even carried own lunch to office - rare especially in Google where there are multiple cafes and elaborate spread
    - Workouts needed to be scheduled in calendar with as much importance as meetings
    - Might have been difficult to do this if working at an early stage startup where things are more unpredictable
    - Had to plan all social outings with friends or anyone else
    - Last 4 months before shoot, every meal, everything that was eating was planned

**The Decision - Connecting it all:**
- At some point in 2023, realized I no longer wanted to work for Google
- Was not getting any kick and didn't feel any growth
- What made Google frustrating:
  - Not any specific moment, but multiple moments where I felt working at Google was too slow
  - Need a lot of approvals to ship even a small thing
  - Moved like an elephant
  - Complex team structures that slowed things down
  - Some politics as well
  - Most people didn't have skin in the game - everyone gets salary regardless of whether project ships
  - Due to all these reasons, I had to do a lot of work to do the actual work
- Was sure I'd leave Google even before getting another job
- The freedom of time and the urge to do something bigger was still there
- Was brainstorming if I could do something on my own
- When the whole fitness thing happened, fitness was the thing I wanted to spend all my time on (I still do)
- Not just my fitness but generally, spending all my time in the fitness space and doing something there
- Solving the problems in that space is what I wanted to do - it became clear to me
- It didn't make any sense to me to spend my time on other things as they didn't appeal to me as much as fitness
- Felt a strong sense of purpose to solve health for other people
- Up to this point, it was also very clear to me that I'd always have a ceiling if I kept working for anyone else even if it's Google, and I have much bigger ambitions than that ceiling

**Practical considerations:**
- Financial situation was mostly good because my wife and I have good amount of savings
- She was still working so we had one income source
- The most hard part of the puzzle was figuring out what I am going to do after I leave
- I still don't have a complete answer but I decided I'd parallely work on two different threads:
  1. Becoming a certified fitness coach
  2. Try to identify opportunities in the fitness space to build software - that's what I had learned in last 10+ years
- It was quite hard to let go of all the unvested shares that I had been granted at Google over all these years

**Reactions and responses:**
- Convincing my family needed a lot of convincing
- Specially due to being from a middle class Indian family where mindset was to always have a stable job
- Wife's reaction: She was quite supportive - thankful for that. We just needed a few brainstorming discussions which actually helped to clear out things in my mind as well
- Last day at Google was quite emotional
  - Before joining Google, it had always been my dream company since I started college
  - Couldn't believe I was actually leaving that company now
  - Had made a good number of friends
  - Hard to digest that I won't be seeing all those faces daily anymore
- Considered alternatives:
  - Initially considered team transfer
  - At some point, it became clear I'd have similar problems working at another team in Google
  - Then at some point, I was sure I didn't want to work for any company for some time
  - Then at some point, I was so sure I want to spend my time on just fitness space for some time
  - So sabbatical too didn't make sense
- What I'm grateful for from Google:
  - Grateful for the people I met there
  - Still think it's one of the best technology companies of our time
  - It's just that I wanted something different from my life
  - Learned a lot in terms of software engineering as well
- Fears and uncertainties:
  - Didn't have a lot of fears as such - thought I could always go back to a job
  - But yes, there was a lot of uncertainty and ambiguity on what I'd actually do
  - It took a lot of time to start defining things and planning what I am going to do
  - Still doing that actually
  - There were always moments of doubt - in a decision like this, you are bound to have doubts
  - But that doubt was not persistent and after a few thoughts, was sure I wanted to do this
